# New research paper (distinct from the earlier recursive graph-construction paper)

**Working title:** Evaluating Markov–TF-IDF Weighted Graph-Augmented Retrieval for Domain-Specific IT Question Answering.

**Research question:** Does weighted graph expansion improve evidence retrieval relative to vector-only and unweighted graph methods on independent reviewed technical questions?

**Ablation protocol:** Hold corpus, query set, embedding model, top-k and model constant; compare vector, unweighted and hybrid; report Recall@K, MRR@K, latency, graph activation, and human-rated answer grounding. Examine failures; do not assume positive gains.

**Submission constraints:** Use university research-paper template and required writing structure; manuscript must be independently human-written according to the supplied guidelines. Do not report results that have not been produced.
